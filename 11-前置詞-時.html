I run a small cafe near the station. On weekdays, we're open from seven to three. On weekends, we open two hours later, but we close at the same time. We're closed on Mondays, and in August, we close for the whole month. My day starts early. A baker brings fresh bread by six, so I get up at five, even on weekends. The busiest time is the morning. Most customers come in before work, around seven thirty, and they want their coffee in two minutes! During lunch, from twelve to one, it's busy again, but after two, the cafe is almost empty. One of my customers is an old man. He comes every day at ten, orders one small coffee, and stays for three hours. On Fridays, he brings his wife, and they stay until closing time. I don't mind. He's always kind, and his stories about the town are funny. After we close, I clean the kitchen for an hour. Then I leave, and I'm usually home in twenty minutes. I'm always tired, but I love my job.

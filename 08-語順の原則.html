When
「いつ」の置き場所
My husband watches TV in the living room on Sundays.
On Sundays, my husband watches TV in the living room.
「いつ」は文の頭にも置ける。そのときは後ろにカンマを付ける。
主語 → 動詞 → 何を の並びは、どちらでも変わらない。

Frequency
頻度の副詞は、一般動詞の前（be動詞なら後ろ）
Kenji plays the guitar on Sundays.
ケンジは日曜日にギターを弾く。
Kenji often plays the guitar on Sundays.
ケンジは日曜日によくギターを弾く。
Kenji is busy.
ケンジは忙しい。
Kenji is often busy.
ケンジは忙しいことが多い。

Manner
様子の副詞は、ふつう動詞の後ろ（「何を」があればその後ろ）
My brother works hard.
兄は一生懸命働く。
The manager reads reports carefully.
部長は報告書を注意深く読む。
The manager carefully reads reports.
部長は報告書を注意深く読む。
carefully のような様子の副詞は、動詞の前にも置ける（hard は置けない）。

My husband, Thomas, and I almost never see each other on weekdays.
I'm a nurse at a hospital across town, and my shift starts at seven,
so I leave the house before six. Thomas is a chef at an Italian restaurant downtown.
He doesn't get home until after midnight, so he sleeps in while I'm at work.
On Mondays, I don't go to the hospital. Instead, I teach a class
at a nursing school near our apartment, and I'm usually home by three.
That's the one afternoon we actually have together,
because the restaurant is closed on Mondays.
Weekends are a different story. Thomas works both days,
and on Sundays he cooks lunch for my parents before his shift.
My mother loves everything he makes. My father, on the other hand,
complains about the sauce every single week,
but he never leaves anything on his plate.

What Is Case
格とは
格 ＝ 名詞が、文の中で動詞とどういう関係にあるか。
格変化 ＝ その関係を、名詞そのものの形で示すしくみ。
casus ＝「倒れること」。まっすぐ立った元の形が主格、ほかの格はそこから倒れた形。

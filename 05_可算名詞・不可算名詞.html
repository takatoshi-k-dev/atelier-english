Plural Forms
複数形の作り方
ふつうは -s
▶
desks  /  chairs  /  days
s, x, ch, sh の後は -es
▶
boxes  /  classes  /  lunches
子音 + y は -ies
▶
copies  /  companies  /  cities
語の終わりで形が決まる

Atelier English  /  05
Be-verbs
不可算名詞と be動詞
可算名詞・1つ
▶
A bookshelf is heavy.
可算名詞・2つ以上
▶
Bookshelves are heavy.
不可算名詞
▶
Furniture is heavy.
不可算名詞は、形の上では単数として扱う
Atelier English  /  05
Action Verbs
不可算名詞と一般動詞
可算名詞・1つ
▶
A bookshelf takes a lot of space.
可算名詞・2つ以上
▶
Bookshelves take a lot of space.
不可算名詞
▶
Furniture takes a lot of space.
形が単数なので、三人称単数の -s が付く

Slowly. I still don't have much information about the new floor.
Woman
Doesn't our landlord have a floor plan?
Man
He has one, but it's out of date. Two of the rooms are different now.
Woman
That's a problem. How many desks will fit in there?
Man
Twelve, maybe thirteen. But we have sixteen people.
Woman
So three of us will have to work from home.
Man
Or we could buy smaller desks. But new furniture isn't cheap.
Woman
And our budget is tight this year. Any advice?
Man
Well, one piece of advice — ask our usual vendor for a quote first.
Woman
Will they give us a discount?
Man
They usually do. What about our old files?
Woman
Put them in boxes. The storage room has six shelves.
Man
Six shelves for twenty years of paperwork?
Woman
Then we'll throw away some of the old paperwork first. I'll start on Monday.

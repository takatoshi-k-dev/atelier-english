Atelier English  /  05
Plural Forms
複数形の作り方
ふつうは -s
▶
desks  /  chairs  /  days
s, x, ch, sh の後は -es
▶
boxes  /  classes  /  lunches
子音 + y は -ies
▶
copies  /  companies  /  cities
語の終わりで形が決まる

Many or Much
たくさん、と言うとき
可算名詞
▶
many desks
不可算名詞
▶
much money
どちらにも
▶
a lot of desks  /  a lot of money
Atelier English  /  05
Much
much を使う場面
I have much money.  ✕
I do not have much money.
Do you have much money?
I have a lot of money.
much は否定文・疑問文で使う。肯定文は a lot of

Every morning, I take my dog for a walk. He wakes me up at six, so I can't sleep late. We go out of the apartment and walk along the river. He loves the river, because there are ducks there. He always runs toward the ducks, but they fly away before he gets close. We never walk across the bridge. It's very noisy, and my dog is afraid of the big trucks on it. After that, we walk through the park. He says hello to every dog in the park, so this part takes a long time. On the way home, we walk past the bakery. The baker likes my dog and gives him a small piece of bread. So he always stops in front of the bakery. Our apartment is on the fourth floor, and he runs up the stairs. I'm always tired at the top, but he isn't!

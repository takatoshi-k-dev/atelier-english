I go to a small gym near my apartment. The trainers there are friendly, but the classes are tough. The woman at the front desk knows everyone by name. My brother and I go there twice a week. Our usual class starts at six and runs for fifty minutes. The people in that class are mostly in their forties. My brother doesn't like the morning classes, so we always take the evening one. The locker room on the second floor isn't crowded in the evening. Our instructor has two kids, so she needs to leave by seven.
